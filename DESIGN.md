# Signal Garden design

## Concept

A botanical oscilloscope used beside an open window after rain. The tension is organic motion produced by rigorous frequency analysis.

## Signature

Sound does not spawn decorative particles. Frequency bands control a deterministic polar growth system: bass changes root mass, mids bend stems, and high frequencies open luminous tips.

## System

- Strategy: drenched black field with moss green as the living material and pale chartreuse for active energy.
- Palette: `oklch(0.08 0 0)` void, `oklch(0.75 0.09 140)` moss, `oklch(0.92 0.17 116)` new growth, `oklch(0.80 0.12 190)` water, `oklch(0.95 0 0)` light.
- Type: Anybody variable for expressive titles; IBM Plex Mono for measurable signal state.
- Layout: the canvas is not inside a card—it is the room. Controls form a slim instrument rail around it.
- Shape: soft radial forms inside a rectilinear frame; small radii only on controls.
- Motion personality: calm fluid. `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`, 120/320/900ms.

## Self-critique

Audio visualizers often become neon equalizers. The growth metaphor is derived from frequency roles and produces a still image worth exporting, so the visual remains meaningful when sound stops.
